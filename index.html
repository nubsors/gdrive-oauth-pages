rclone Google Drive Access

Private, single-user OAuth application

This application exists solely to let its owner connect the rclone file-transfer tool to their own Google Drive account over the Google Drive API.

What it does

  • Authenticates a single user to their own Google Drive.
  • Mounts, lists, uploads, and downloads files within that account.
  • Runs locally on the owner's own machine. There is no server and no third party.

What it does not do

  • It does not serve any other users, and it has no public sign-up.
  • It does not collect, transmit, or store data belonging to anyone else.
  • It does not share data with any third party.

The only scope requested is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive, used exclusively against the owner's own account.
